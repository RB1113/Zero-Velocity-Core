About Zero Velocity Core

Zero Velocity Core (ZVC) is a precision‑engineered aim‑training system built for players who want every shot to feel intentional. Each drill refines micro‑adjustments, sharpens reaction timing, strengthens tracking control, and eliminates wasted movement — all inside one unified training environment.

ZVC pushes your mechanics toward a state of controlled stability: zero hesitation, zero drift, zero wasted motion. Just clean, disciplined, competitive aim.

It’s not a game. It’s not a warm‑up. It’s a core — built to make your aim feel effortless, repeatable, and dangerous.

Zero Velocity Core (ZVC) is a precision‑built aim‑training system engineered for players who want every shot to matter. Why ZVC?

ZVC isn’t about flashy effects or gimmicks — it’s about building a repeatable, stable foundation for your aim. Every drill exists to reveal something: inconsistency, over‑correction, hesitation, or wasted motion. The goal is simple: turn your mechanics into something you can trust under pressure.
